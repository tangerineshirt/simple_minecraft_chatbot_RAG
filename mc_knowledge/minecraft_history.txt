# Minecraft History Knowledge Base

## Minecraft History
Minecraft was created by Markus Persson, also known as Notch, and early public versions appeared in 2009. The game grew from a small sandbox building project into one of the most popular games in the world. Mojang continued developing Minecraft, and Microsoft later acquired Mojang in 2014. Over time, Minecraft expanded with survival mechanics, multiplayer, dimensions such as the Nether and the End, new mobs, biomes, redstone systems, villages, and many major updates.

## Minecraft Development
Minecraft began as an indie sandbox game focused on block placement, mining, and procedural worlds. Early development added survival mode, crafting, hostile mobs, multiplayer, and world generation improvements. Later updates expanded the game with new dimensions, bosses, structures, villagers, redstone mechanics, aquatic content, caves, cliffs, and trial chambers.

## Minecraft Editions
Minecraft has multiple editions. Java Edition is the original PC version and is known for modding, servers, snapshots, and technical features. Bedrock Edition is designed for cross-platform play across consoles, mobile, and Windows. Some mechanics, commands, redstone behavior, combat behavior, and features can differ between Java Edition and Bedrock Edition.
