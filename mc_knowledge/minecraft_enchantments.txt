# Minecraft Enchantments Knowledge Base

## Aqua Affinity
Category: Armor enchantment.
Applies to: Helmet.
Maximum level: I.
Effect: Increases underwater mining speed.
Useful for: Mining blocks underwater more comfortably.
Notes: Aqua Affinity is useful when exploring oceans, underwater ruins, shipwrecks, or building underwater bases.

## Bane of Arthropods
Category: Weapon enchantment.
Applies to: Sword and axe.
Maximum level: V.
Effect: Increases damage dealt to arthropod mobs.
Useful for: Fighting spiders, cave spiders, silverfish, endermites, and bees.
Incompatible with: Sharpness, Smite, Density, and Breach depending on the item/version.
Notes: This enchantment is very situational because it only helps against arthropod-type mobs.

## Blast Protection
Category: Armor enchantment.
Applies to: Armor.
Maximum level: IV.
Effect: Reduces damage from explosions.
Useful for: Surviving creepers, TNT, beds exploding in the Nether or End, respawn anchors, and ghast fireballs.
Incompatible with: Protection, Fire Protection, and Projectile Protection.
Notes: Blast Protection is useful if the player often fights creepers or works with explosives.

## Breach
Category: Mace enchantment.
Applies to: Mace.
Maximum level: IV.
Effect: Reduces the effectiveness of the target's armor when hit with a mace.
Useful for: Fighting armored enemies.
Incompatible with: Density, Smite, and Bane of Arthropods.
Notes: Breach is mainly useful for mace combat.

## Channeling
Category: Trident enchantment.
Applies to: Trident.
Maximum level: I.
Effect: Summons lightning when a thrown trident hits a mob during a thunderstorm.
Useful for: Creating charged creepers, converting villagers into witches, converting pigs into zombified piglins, and using lightning-based effects.
Incompatible with: Riptide.
Notes: Channeling only works during thunderstorms and when the target is exposed to the sky.

## Curse of Binding
Category: Curse enchantment.
Applies to: Wearable items such as armor.
Maximum level: I.
Effect: Prevents the cursed item from being removed after it is equipped.
Useful for: Mostly used as a trap or challenge mechanic.
Notes: The item usually cannot be removed until it breaks or the player dies, depending on game rules and mode.

## Curse of Vanishing
Category: Curse enchantment.
Applies to: Most enchantable items.
Maximum level: I.
Effect: Causes the item to disappear when the player dies.
Useful for: Mostly used as a curse or challenge mechanic.
Notes: Curse of Vanishing is usually undesirable because it makes items permanently disappear after death.

## Density
Category: Mace enchantment.
Applies to: Mace.
Maximum level: V.
Effect: Increases mace damage based on falling distance.
Useful for: Attacking enemies after falling from a height.
Incompatible with: Breach, Smite, and Bane of Arthropods.
Notes: Density is useful for players who use the mace's smash attack mechanics.

## Depth Strider
Category: Armor enchantment.
Applies to: Boots.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Increases underwater movement speed.
Useful for: Swimming, underwater exploration, ocean monuments, and underwater building.
Incompatible with: Frost Walker.
Notes: Depth Strider makes movement underwater much less slow.

## Efficiency
Category: Tool enchantment.
Applies to: Pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, and shears.
Maximum level: V.
Effect: Increases mining or block-breaking speed.
Useful for: Mining, digging, chopping trees, and general resource gathering.
Notes: Efficiency V is one of the most useful tool enchantments for long-term survival gameplay.

## Feather Falling
Category: Armor enchantment.
Applies to: Boots.
Maximum level: IV.
Effect: Reduces fall damage.
Useful for: Exploring mountains, caves, the End, and tall structures.
Notes: Feather Falling is one of the best boot enchantments because fall damage is a common cause of death.

## Fire Aspect
Category: Weapon enchantment.
Applies to: Sword.
Maximum level: II.
Effect: Sets targets on fire when hit.
Useful for: Dealing extra fire damage and cooking some animal drops automatically.
Notes: Fire Aspect can be annoying when fighting Endermen because burning Endermen may teleport away.

## Fire Protection
Category: Armor enchantment.
Applies to: Armor.
Maximum level: IV.
Effect: Reduces fire and lava damage.
Useful for: Nether exploration, fighting blazes, and surviving lava accidents.
Incompatible with: Protection, Blast Protection, and Projectile Protection.
Notes: Fire Protection is useful in the Nether, but general Protection is usually more flexible.

## Flame
Category: Bow enchantment.
Applies to: Bow.
Maximum level: I.
Effect: Makes arrows set targets on fire.
Useful for: Burning mobs from a distance and dealing extra damage over time.
Notes: Flame does not work the same as Fire Aspect because it is specifically for bows.

## Fortune
Category: Tool enchantment.
Applies to: Pickaxe, axe, shovel, and hoe.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Increases drops from certain blocks.
Useful for: Mining diamonds, coal, emeralds, lapis lazuli, redstone, nether quartz, and some crops.
Incompatible with: Silk Touch.
Notes: Fortune III is highly valuable for mining ores that drop items instead of blocks.

## Frost Walker
Category: Armor enchantment.
Applies to: Boots.
Maximum level: II.
Effect: Freezes nearby water into temporary frosted ice when the player walks over it.
Useful for: Crossing oceans, rivers, and lakes without a boat.
Incompatible with: Depth Strider.
Notes: Frost Walker is useful for travel but can be inconvenient near farms or water builds.

## Impaling
Category: Trident enchantment.
Applies to: Trident.
Maximum level: V.
Effect: Increases trident damage against aquatic mobs or wet targets depending on the edition.
Useful for: Ocean combat and trident fighting.
Notes: The exact behavior of Impaling can differ between Java Edition and Bedrock Edition.

## Infinity
Category: Bow enchantment.
Applies to: Bow.
Maximum level: I.
Effect: Allows the bow to shoot arrows without consuming normal arrows, as long as the player has at least one arrow.
Useful for: Long-range combat without needing many arrows.
Incompatible with: Mending.
Notes: Infinity does not apply to special arrows such as tipped arrows or spectral arrows in the same way as normal arrows.

## Knockback
Category: Weapon enchantment.
Applies to: Sword.
Maximum level: II.
Effect: Increases the distance enemies are knocked back when hit.
Useful for: Keeping mobs away from the player.
Notes: Knockback can be useful defensively, but it can also make combat annoying by pushing enemies out of reach.

## Looting
Category: Weapon enchantment.
Applies to: Sword.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Increases the number or chance of drops from mobs.
Useful for: Farming mob drops such as ender pearls, blaze rods, gunpowder, bones, string, and rare drops.
Notes: Looting III is one of the best sword enchantments for resource farming.

## Loyalty
Category: Trident enchantment.
Applies to: Trident.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Makes a thrown trident return to the player.
Useful for: Using a trident as a reusable ranged weapon.
Incompatible with: Riptide.
Notes: Higher levels make the trident return faster.

## Luck of the Sea
Category: Fishing rod enchantment.
Applies to: Fishing rod.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Increases the chance of getting treasure while fishing.
Useful for: Fishing for enchanted books, name tags, saddles, and other treasure items.
Notes: Luck of the Sea is usually paired with Lure for better fishing.

## Lure
Category: Fishing rod enchantment.
Applies to: Fishing rod.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Decreases the waiting time before fish bite.
Useful for: Faster fishing.
Notes: Lure helps speed up fishing sessions.

## Mending
Category: Treasure enchantment.
Applies to: Many tools, weapons, and armor pieces.
Maximum level: I.
Effect: Uses experience orbs to repair the item instead of adding experience to the player's level.
Useful for: Keeping valuable gear repaired permanently.
Incompatible with: Infinity on bows.
Notes: Mending is one of the most valuable enchantments in survival mode.

## Multishot
Category: Crossbow enchantment.
Applies to: Crossbow.
Maximum level: I.
Effect: Shoots three projectiles at once while consuming only one projectile.
Useful for: Hitting multiple enemies or increasing spread.
Incompatible with: Piercing.
Notes: Multishot works with arrows and firework rockets.

## Piercing
Category: Crossbow enchantment.
Applies to: Crossbow.
Maximum level: IV.
Effect: Allows arrows fired from a crossbow to pass through multiple entities.
Useful for: Fighting groups of enemies in a line.
Incompatible with: Multishot.
Notes: Higher levels allow the projectile to pierce through more targets.

## Power
Category: Bow enchantment.
Applies to: Bow.
Maximum level: V.
Effect: Increases arrow damage.
Useful for: Stronger ranged attacks.
Notes: Power V is one of the most important bow enchantments.

## Projectile Protection
Category: Armor enchantment.
Applies to: Armor.
Maximum level: IV.
Effect: Reduces damage from projectiles.
Useful for: Fighting skeletons, pillagers, ghasts, shulkers, and other ranged attackers.
Incompatible with: Protection, Fire Protection, and Blast Protection.
Notes: Projectile Protection is useful in ranged combat, but general Protection is more flexible.

## Protection
Category: Armor enchantment.
Applies to: Armor.
Maximum level: IV.
Effect: Reduces many types of damage.
Useful for: General survival protection.
Incompatible with: Fire Protection, Blast Protection, and Projectile Protection.
Notes: Protection IV is usually the most flexible armor protection enchantment.

## Punch
Category: Bow enchantment.
Applies to: Bow.
Maximum level: II.
Effect: Increases arrow knockback.
Useful for: Keeping enemies away from the player.
Notes: Punch can be useful defensively, but it can also push targets farther away.

## Quick Charge
Category: Crossbow enchantment.
Applies to: Crossbow.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Reduces crossbow reload time.
Useful for: Firing crossbows more quickly.
Notes: Quick Charge III makes the crossbow much faster to use.

## Respiration
Category: Armor enchantment.
Applies to: Helmet.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Extends underwater breathing time.
Useful for: Underwater exploration, ocean monuments, and underwater building.
Notes: Respiration is often paired with Aqua Affinity.

## Riptide
Category: Trident enchantment.
Applies to: Trident.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Launches the player forward when the trident is used in water or rain.
Useful for: Fast travel in rain, oceans, and watery areas.
Incompatible with: Channeling and Loyalty.
Notes: Riptide changes the trident from a thrown weapon into a mobility tool.

## Sharpness
Category: Weapon enchantment.
Applies to: Sword and axe.
Maximum level: V.
Effect: Increases general melee damage.
Useful for: Fighting most mobs and players.
Incompatible with: Smite, Bane of Arthropods, Density, and Breach depending on the item/version.
Notes: Sharpness is the most general-purpose damage enchantment.

## Silk Touch
Category: Tool enchantment.
Applies to: Pickaxe, axe, shovel, and hoe.
Maximum level: I.
Effect: Makes blocks drop themselves instead of their usual drops.
Useful for: Collecting blocks such as glass, ice, bookshelves, grass blocks, ores, and other special blocks.
Incompatible with: Fortune.
Notes: Silk Touch is useful when the player wants the original block instead of its drops.

## Smite
Category: Weapon enchantment.
Applies to: Sword and axe.
Maximum level: V.
Effect: Increases damage against undead mobs.
Useful for: Fighting zombies, skeletons, wither skeletons, drowned, husks, strays, zombified piglins, phantoms, and the Wither.
Incompatible with: Sharpness, Bane of Arthropods, Density, and Breach depending on the item/version.
Notes: Smite V is very strong against undead mobs.

## Soul Speed
Category: Treasure armor enchantment.
Applies to: Boots.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Increases movement speed on soul sand and soul soil.
Useful for: Nether travel, especially in soul sand valleys.
Notes: Soul Speed can reduce boot durability while used.

## Sweeping Edge
Category: Weapon enchantment.
Applies to: Sword.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Increases sweeping attack damage.
Useful for: Fighting groups of mobs.
Notes: Sweeping Edge is available in Java Edition and is related to sword sweep attacks.

## Swift Sneak
Category: Treasure armor enchantment.
Applies to: Leggings.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Increases movement speed while sneaking.
Useful for: Ancient cities, bridging, building, and moving carefully without standing up.
Notes: Swift Sneak is especially useful in the Deep Dark where sneaking helps avoid vibrations.

## Thorns
Category: Armor enchantment.
Applies to: Armor.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Gives attackers a chance to take damage when they hit the wearer.
Useful for: Passive retaliation against enemies.
Notes: Thorns can reduce armor durability faster.

## Unbreaking
Category: Durability enchantment.
Applies to: Most tools, weapons, and armor.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Increases item durability by giving the item a chance not to lose durability when used.
Useful for: Making gear last longer.
Notes: Unbreaking III is useful on almost every important item.

## Wind Burst
Category: Mace enchantment.
Applies to: Mace.
Maximum level: III.
Effect: Launches the player upward after hitting an enemy with a mace smash attack.
Useful for: Chaining mace attacks and mobility after successful smash hits.
Notes: Wind Burst is a mace-specific enchantment and is useful for advanced mace movement and combat.
