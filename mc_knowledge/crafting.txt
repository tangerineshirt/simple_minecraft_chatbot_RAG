# Minecraft Crafting Recipes Knowledge Base

## Crafting Table
A crafting table is crafted using 4 wooden planks.
Place 4 wooden planks in a 2x2 crafting grid.

## Wooden Planks
Wooden planks are crafted from logs.
1 log creates 4 wooden planks.

## Sticks
Sticks are crafted using 2 wooden planks.
Place 2 wooden planks vertically to create 4 sticks.

## Torch
A torch is crafted using 1 stick and 1 coal or charcoal.
Place coal or charcoal above a stick.

## Furnace
A furnace is crafted using 8 cobblestone.
Place cobblestone around the crafting grid and leave the center empty.

## Chest
A chest is crafted using 8 wooden planks.
Place wooden planks around the crafting grid and leave the center empty.

## Door
A wooden door is crafted using 6 wooden planks.
Place the planks in two vertical columns.

## Ladder
A ladder is crafted using 7 sticks.
Place sticks on the left and right columns, and one stick in the center.

## Bed
A bed is crafted using 3 wool and 3 wooden planks.
Place 3 wool in the top row and 3 wooden planks below them.
The wool must be the same color.

## Shield
A shield is crafted using 6 wooden planks and 1 iron ingot.
Place the iron ingot in the top middle slot and arrange the planks around it.

## Bow
A bow is crafted using 3 sticks and 3 strings.
Place sticks in a curved shape and strings in a vertical line.

## Arrow
Arrows are crafted using 1 flint, 1 stick, and 1 feather.
Place flint on top, stick in the middle, and feather at the bottom.
This creates 4 arrows.

## Sword
A sword is crafted using 1 stick and 2 material items.
The material can be wood planks, stone, iron ingots, gold ingots, diamonds, or netherite.
Place 2 material items vertically above 1 stick.

## Pickaxe
A pickaxe is crafted using 3 material items and 2 sticks.
Place 3 material items across the top row and 2 sticks vertically in the center.
The material can be wood planks, cobblestone, iron ingots, gold ingots, diamonds, or netherite.

## Axe
An axe is crafted using 3 material items and 2 sticks.
Place the material items in an upper corner shape and place 2 sticks vertically below.
The material can be wood planks, cobblestone, iron ingots, gold ingots, diamonds, or netherite.

## Shovel
A shovel is crafted using 1 material item and 2 sticks.
Place the material item on top and 2 sticks vertically below.
The material can be wood planks, cobblestone, iron ingots, gold ingots, diamonds, or netherite.

## Hoe
A hoe is crafted using 2 material items and 2 sticks.
Place 2 material items on the top row and 2 sticks vertically below.
The material can be wood planks, cobblestone, iron ingots, gold ingots, diamonds, or netherite.

## Helmet
A helmet is crafted using 5 armor materials.
Place 3 materials on the top row and 2 materials on the left and right of the middle row.
The material can be leather, iron ingots, gold ingots, diamonds, or netherite.

## Chestplate
A chestplate is crafted using 8 armor materials.
Fill every slot except the top middle slot.
The material can be leather, iron ingots, gold ingots, diamonds, or netherite.

## Leggings
Leggings are crafted using 7 armor materials.
Fill the top row and the left and right columns, leaving the center and bottom middle empty.
The material can be leather, iron ingots, gold ingots, diamonds, or netherite.

## Boots
Boots are crafted using 4 armor materials.
Place materials in the bottom left, bottom right, middle left, and middle right slots.
The material can be leather, iron ingots, gold ingots, diamonds, or netherite.

## Bucket
A bucket is crafted using 3 iron ingots.
Place iron ingots in a V shape.

## Shears
Shears are crafted using 2 iron ingots.
Place the iron ingots diagonally.

## Flint and Steel
Flint and steel is crafted using 1 iron ingot and 1 flint.
Place flint and an iron ingot diagonally.

## Compass
A compass is crafted using 4 iron ingots and 1 redstone dust.
Place redstone dust in the center and iron ingots above, below, left, and right of it.

## Clock
A clock is crafted using 4 gold ingots and 1 redstone dust.
Place redstone dust in the center and gold ingots above, below, left, and right of it.

## Map
A map is crafted using 8 paper and 1 compass.
Place the compass in the center and surround it with paper.

## Paper
Paper is crafted using 3 sugar cane.
Place 3 sugar cane in a horizontal row.
This creates 3 paper.

## Book
A book is crafted using 3 paper and 1 leather.

## Bookshelf
A bookshelf is crafted using 6 wooden planks and 3 books.
Place 3 planks on the top row, 3 books in the middle row, and 3 planks on the bottom row.

## Enchantment Table
An enchantment table is crafted using 4 obsidian, 2 diamonds, and 1 book.
Place the book in the top middle slot, diamonds on the left and right of the middle row, obsidian in the center, and 3 obsidian on the bottom row.

## Nether Portal
Category: Structure.
Type: Portal.
Built with: Obsidian blocks.
Activation item: Flint and Steel.
Minimum frame: 10 obsidian blocks if the corner blocks are omitted.
Full frame: 14 obsidian blocks if the corner blocks are included.
How to build: Build a vertical rectangular obsidian frame. The minimum frame has 2 obsidian blocks on the bottom, 2 obsidian blocks on the top, and 3 obsidian blocks on each side. After the frame is built, use flint and steel on the inside of the frame to activate the portal.
Important note: A Nether portal is not crafted in a crafting table.
Use: Allows travel between the Overworld and the Nether.

## Bread
Bread is crafted using 3 wheat.
Place 3 wheat in a horizontal row.

## Cake
A cake is crafted using 3 milk buckets, 2 sugar, 1 egg, and 3 wheat.
Place milk buckets on the top row, sugar on the left and right of the middle row, egg in the center, and wheat on the bottom row.

## Cookie
Cookies are crafted using 2 wheat and 1 cocoa bean.
Place the cocoa bean between the wheat.
This creates 8 cookies.

## Fishing Rod
A fishing rod is crafted using 3 sticks and 2 strings.
Place sticks diagonally and strings down from the top-right side.

## Boat
A boat is crafted using 5 wooden planks.
Place planks in a U shape.

## Minecart
A minecart is crafted using 5 iron ingots.
Place iron ingots in a U shape.

## Rail
Rails are crafted using 6 iron ingots and 1 stick.
Place iron ingots on the left and right columns and a stick in the center.
This creates 16 rails.

## Powered Rail
Powered rails are crafted using 6 gold ingots, 1 stick, and 1 redstone dust.
Place gold ingots on the left and right columns, a stick in the center, and redstone dust at the bottom middle.
This creates 6 powered rails.

## Redstone Torch
A redstone torch is crafted using 1 redstone dust and 1 stick.
Place redstone dust above a stick.

## Lever
A lever is crafted using 1 stick and 1 cobblestone.
Place the stick above the cobblestone.

## Button
A button can be crafted from 1 stone or 1 wooden plank.
Stone creates a stone button.
Wooden planks create a wooden button.

## Pressure Plate
A pressure plate is crafted using 2 matching blocks or planks placed side by side.
Wooden planks create a wooden pressure plate.
Stone creates a stone pressure plate.

## Trapdoor
A trapdoor is crafted using 6 wooden planks.
Place planks in two horizontal rows.
This creates 2 trapdoors.

## Fence
A fence is crafted using wooden planks and sticks.
Place sticks in the center column and wooden planks on both sides.
This creates 3 fences.

## Fence Gate
A fence gate is crafted using 2 wooden planks and 4 sticks.
Place planks in the center column and sticks on both sides.

## Sign
A sign is crafted using 6 wooden planks and 1 stick.
Place 6 planks in the top two rows and a stick in the bottom middle.
This creates 3 signs.

## Barrel
A barrel is crafted using sticks and wooden slabs in Java Edition.
In Java Edition, place wooden slabs in the top middle and bottom middle, with sticks on the sides.
Recipes may differ between Java Edition and Bedrock Edition.

## Campfire
A campfire is crafted using 3 sticks, 1 coal or charcoal, and 3 logs.
Place coal or charcoal in the center, sticks around it, and logs on the bottom row.

## Anvil
An anvil is crafted using 3 iron blocks and 4 iron ingots.
Place 3 iron blocks on the top row, 1 iron ingot in the center, and 3 iron ingots on the bottom row.

## Blast Furnace
A blast furnace is crafted using 5 iron ingots, 1 furnace, and 3 smooth stone.
Place the furnace in the center, smooth stone on the bottom row, and iron ingots around the top and sides.

## Smoker
A smoker is crafted using 1 furnace and 4 logs or stems.
Place the furnace in the center and logs above, below, left, and right of it.

## Grindstone
A grindstone is crafted using 2 sticks, 1 stone slab, and 2 wooden planks.
Place the stone slab in the top middle, sticks on the top left and top right, and planks below the sticks.

## Stonecutter
A stonecutter is crafted using 1 iron ingot and 3 stone.
Place the iron ingot in the center and 3 stone on the bottom row.

## Smithing Table
A smithing table is crafted using 2 iron ingots and 4 wooden planks.
Place iron ingots on the top row and wooden planks below.

## Cartography Table
A cartography table is crafted using 2 paper and 4 wooden planks.
Place paper on the top row and wooden planks below.

## Fletching Table
A fletching table is crafted using 2 flint and 4 wooden planks.
Place flint on the top row and wooden planks below.

## Loom
A loom is crafted using 2 strings and 2 wooden planks.
Place strings on the top row and planks below.

## Composter
A composter is crafted using wooden slabs.
Place wooden slabs in a U shape.

## Beehive
A beehive is crafted using 6 wooden planks and 3 honeycomb.
Place planks on the top and bottom rows and honeycomb in the middle row.

## TNT
TNT is crafted using 5 gunpowder and 4 sand or red sand.
Place gunpowder and sand in an alternating pattern.

## Firework Rocket
A firework rocket is crafted using paper and gunpowder.
Optional firework stars can be added for explosion effects.

## Firework Star
A firework star is crafted using gunpowder and dye.
Additional ingredients can change the firework effect.

## Eye of Ender
An eye of ender is crafted using 1 ender pearl and 1 blaze powder.

## Blaze Powder
Blaze powder is crafted from blaze rods.
1 blaze rod creates 2 blaze powder.

## Ender Chest
An ender chest is crafted using 8 obsidian and 1 eye of ender.
Place the eye of ender in the center and surround it with obsidian.

## Beacon
A beacon is crafted using 5 glass, 3 obsidian, and 1 nether star.
Place glass on the top row, glass on the left and right of the middle row, the nether star in the center, and obsidian on the bottom row.
