# Minecraft Potions Knowledge Base

## Brewing Basics
Category: Potion system.
Purpose: Brewing is the process of creating potions using a brewing stand.
Required equipment: Brewing stand, blaze powder, glass bottles, water, and brewing ingredients.
Basic process: Fill glass bottles with water, place them in the brewing stand, add blaze powder as fuel, then add brewing ingredients.
Notes: Most useful potions start from a Water Bottle that is brewed with Nether Wart to create an Awkward Potion.

## Brewing Stand
Category: Brewing equipment.
Use: A brewing stand is used to brew ingredients into water bottles or existing potions.
Fuel: Blaze powder.
Notes: Without blaze powder, the brewing stand cannot brew potions.

## Blaze Powder
Category: Brewing fuel and ingredient.
Use as fuel: Blaze powder powers the brewing stand.
Use as ingredient: Blaze powder can also be used as an ingredient to create a Potion of Strength.
Source: Crafted from blaze rods.
Notes: Blaze rods are dropped by blazes, which are commonly found in Nether fortresses.

## Water Bottle
Category: Base potion.
Created from: Filling a glass bottle with water.
Effect: No potion effect.
Use: Starting base for potion brewing.
Notes: Water Bottles are usually brewed with Nether Wart to create Awkward Potions.

## Awkward Potion
Category: Base potion.
Created from: Water Bottle plus Nether Wart.
Effect: No direct potion effect.
Use: Main base for most useful potions.
Notes: Most positive and negative effect potions are brewed from an Awkward Potion.

## Mundane Potion
Category: Base potion.
Created from: Water Bottle plus certain ingredients such as redstone dust.
Effect: No useful effect.
Use: Limited brewing use.
Notes: Mundane Potions are generally not the main base for useful potions.

## Thick Potion
Category: Base potion.
Created from: Water Bottle plus glowstone dust.
Effect: No useful effect.
Use: Limited brewing use.
Notes: Thick Potions are generally not the main base for useful potions.

## Redstone Dust Modifier
Category: Potion modifier.
Effect: Extends the duration of many potions.
Use: Add redstone dust to a compatible potion to make the effect last longer.
Example: Potion of Night Vision can be extended with redstone dust.
Notes: Redstone usually increases duration, but it does not increase potion strength.

## Glowstone Dust Modifier
Category: Potion modifier.
Effect: Increases the strength or level of some potions.
Use: Add glowstone dust to a compatible potion to create a stronger version.
Example: Potion of Healing can become Potion of Healing II.
Notes: Glowstone usually makes a potion stronger but may prevent it from being extended with redstone.

## Gunpowder Modifier
Category: Potion modifier.
Effect: Turns a drinkable potion into a splash potion.
Use: Add gunpowder to a potion.
Result: Splash Potion.
Notes: Splash potions can be thrown and can affect mobs, players, or an area.

## Dragon's Breath Modifier
Category: Potion modifier.
Effect: Turns a splash potion into a lingering potion.
Use: Add dragon's breath to a splash potion.
Result: Lingering Potion.
Notes: Lingering potions create an effect cloud that remains on the ground for a short time.

## Fermented Spider Eye Modifier
Category: Potion modifier.
Effect: Corrupts or changes certain potions into different effects.
Use: Add fermented spider eye to compatible potions.
Examples: Night Vision can become Invisibility. Healing can become Harming. Swiftness can become Slowness.
Notes: Fermented spider eye is also used to create Potion of Weakness directly from a Water Bottle.

## Splash Potion
Category: Potion form.
Created from: Any compatible potion plus gunpowder.
Effect: Can be thrown to apply the potion effect in an area.
Useful for: Affecting mobs, enemies, teammates, or multiple targets.
Notes: Splash potions are useful for combat, traps, multiplayer support, and applying effects without drinking.

## Lingering Potion
Category: Potion form.
Created from: Splash Potion plus dragon's breath.
Effect: Creates a lingering effect cloud on the ground.
Useful for: Area control, traps, multiplayer support, and crafting tipped arrows.
Notes: Lingering potions usually have shorter effective duration than normal drinkable potions.

## Potion of Healing
Category: Positive potion.
Base potion: Awkward Potion.
Main ingredient: Glistering Melon Slice.
Effect: Instantly restores health.
Can be enhanced: Yes, with glowstone dust.
Can be extended: No.
Useful for: Quickly recovering health during combat.
Notes: Potion of Healing damages undead mobs because undead mobs are harmed by healing effects.

## Potion of Harming
Category: Negative potion.
Base potion: Potion of Healing or Potion of Poison.
Main ingredient: Fermented Spider Eye.
Effect: Instantly damages the target.
Can be enhanced: Yes, with glowstone dust.
Can be extended: No.
Useful for: Damaging enemies quickly, especially as a splash potion.
Notes: Potion of Harming heals undead mobs because undead mobs react oppositely to instant damage and healing effects.

## Potion of Regeneration
Category: Positive potion.
Base potion: Awkward Potion.
Main ingredient: Ghast Tear.
Effect: Gradually restores health over time.
Can be enhanced: Yes, with glowstone dust.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Boss fights, raids, PvP, and dangerous exploration.
Notes: Regeneration is useful when the player expects to take repeated damage.

## Potion of Strength
Category: Positive potion.
Base potion: Awkward Potion.
Main ingredient: Blaze Powder.
Effect: Increases melee attack damage.
Can be enhanced: Yes, with glowstone dust.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Fighting bosses, hostile mobs, raids, and players.
Notes: Strength is one of the strongest combat potions for melee fighting.

## Potion of Swiftness
Category: Positive potion.
Base potion: Awkward Potion.
Main ingredient: Sugar.
Effect: Increases movement speed.
Can be enhanced: Yes, with glowstone dust.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Traveling, escaping danger, exploring, and PvP.
Notes: Swiftness helps the player move faster on land.

## Potion of Slowness
Category: Negative potion.
Base potion: Potion of Swiftness or Potion of Leaping.
Main ingredient: Fermented Spider Eye.
Effect: Reduces movement speed.
Can be enhanced: Yes, with glowstone dust in some versions.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Slowing enemies, traps, PvP, and mob control.
Notes: Slowness is usually most useful as a splash potion.

## Potion of Leaping
Category: Positive potion.
Base potion: Awkward Potion.
Main ingredient: Rabbit's Foot.
Effect: Increases jump height and reduces fall damage slightly.
Can be enhanced: Yes, with glowstone dust.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Traversing rough terrain, jumping over obstacles, and exploration.
Notes: Leaping can help with movement, but it is more situational than Swiftness.

## Potion of Fire Resistance
Category: Positive potion.
Base potion: Awkward Potion.
Main ingredient: Magma Cream.
Effect: Protects the player from fire and lava damage.
Can be enhanced: No.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Nether exploration, lava lakes, fighting blazes, and mining near lava.
Notes: Fire Resistance is one of the most useful potions for surviving in the Nether.

Base potion: Awkward Potion.

Effect: Allows the player to breathe underwater.
Can be enhanced: No.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Ocean monuments, underwater ruins, shipwrecks, underwater caves, and ocean building.
Notes: Water Breathing is very useful when combined with Respiration and Aqua Affinity gear.

## Potion of Night Vision
Category: Positive potion.
Base potion: Awkward Potion.
Main ingredient: Golden Carrot.
Effect: Allows the player to see clearly in darkness and underwater.
Can be enhanced: No.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.

Notes: Night Vision makes dark areas much easier to navigate.

## Potion of Invisibility
Category: Positive utility potion.
Base potion: Potion of Night Vision.
Main ingredient: Fermented Spider Eye.
Effect: Makes the player invisible.
Can be enhanced: No.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Avoiding mobs, sneaking around, multiplayer tricks, and stealth.

## Potion of Poison
Category: Negative potion.
Base potion: Awkward Potion.
Main ingredient: Spider Eye.
Effect: Gradually reduces health over time but usually does not kill by itself.
Can be enhanced: Yes, with glowstone dust.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Weakening enemies before finishing them.
Notes: Poison is usually best used as a splash potion against living targets.

## Potion of Weakness
Category: Negative potion.
Base potion: Water Bottle or Awkward Potion depending on recipe path.
Main ingredient: Fermented Spider Eye.
Effect: Reduces melee attack damage.
Can be enhanced: No.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Weakening enemies and curing zombie villagers.
Notes: To cure a zombie villager, apply Weakness and then give it a golden apple.

## Potion of Slow Falling
Category: Positive potion.
Base potion: Awkward Potion.
Main ingredient: Phantom Membrane.
Effect: Makes the player fall slowly and prevents fall damage.
Can be enhanced: No.
Can be extended: Yes, with redstone dust.
Useful for: Exploring the End, fighting the Ender Dragon, building high structures, and surviving falls.
Notes: Slow Falling is extremely useful in the End because it helps prevent death from being knocked into the air.

## Brewing Helpful Potion Order
Category: Brewing guide.
Recommended order: Start with Water Bottles, add Nether Wart to create Awkward Potions, then add the main ingredient for the desired effect.
Example: Water Bottle plus Nether Wart creates Awkward Potion. Awkward Potion plus Magma Cream creates Potion of Fire Resistance.
Notes: Add redstone for longer duration, glowstone for stronger effect, gunpowder for splash form, and dragon's breath for lingering form.

## Potion Brewing Safety Tips
Category: Brewing guide.
Tip: Use Fire Resistance before exploring lava-heavy Nether areas.
Tip: Use Water Breathing before exploring ocean monuments or underwater caves.
Tip: Use Slow Falling before fighting the Ender Dragon.
Tip: Use Strength and Regeneration before boss fights.
Tip: Use Splash Weakness and a golden apple to cure zombie villagers.
Notes: Potions are most useful when prepared before dangerous situations.
