# Minecraft Mobs Knowledge Base

## Creeper
Type: Hostile mob.
Common location: Dark areas in the Overworld, especially at night or in unlit spaces.
Behavior: Creepers silently approach players and explode after getting close. Their explosion can damage players, mobs, and blocks.
Drops: Gunpowder. If killed by a skeleton or stray, a creeper can drop a music disc. A charged creeper can cause certain mobs to drop mob heads.
Tips: Keep distance, use a shield to reduce explosion damage, light up areas to prevent spawning, and avoid letting creepers approach buildings.

## Zombie
Type: Hostile undead mob.
Common location: Dark areas in the Overworld.
Behavior: Zombies walk toward players and attack in melee range. They can burn in sunlight unless protected by shade, water, or equipment.
Drops: Rotten flesh. They can rarely drop iron ingots, carrots, potatoes, or equipped items.
Tips: Use a sword, axe, bow, or shield. Keep villages protected because zombies can attack villagers.

## Skeleton
Type: Hostile undead mob.
Common location: Dark areas in the Overworld.
Behavior: Skeletons attack players from a distance using bows. They can burn in sunlight unless protected by shade, water, or equipment.
Drops: Bones, arrows, and sometimes bows.
Tips: Use a shield to block arrows, close the distance carefully, or attack with a bow.

## Spider
Type: Neutral or hostile mob depending on light level.
Common location: Overworld, especially at night or in dark areas.
Behavior: Spiders climb walls and attack players in low light. In bright light, they may become neutral unless attacked.
Drops: String and spider eyes.
Tips: Be careful near walls and caves because spiders can climb over obstacles.

## Cave Spider
Type: Hostile mob.
Common location: Mineshafts, usually from cave spider spawners.
Behavior: Cave spiders are smaller than normal spiders and can poison players.
Drops: String and spider eyes.
Tips: Carry milk to remove poison and block spawner areas carefully.

## Enderman
Type: Neutral mob.
Common location: Overworld, Nether, and End dimensions.
Behavior: Endermen are tall mobs that teleport. They become hostile if the player looks directly at their face or attacks them.
Drops: Ender pearls.
Tips: Avoid eye contact, fight under a two-block-high shelter, or use water because Endermen avoid water.

## Witch
Type: Hostile mob.
Common location: Swamp huts, raids, and dark areas.
Behavior: Witches throw harmful splash potions and drink potions to heal or protect themselves.
Drops: Glowstone dust, redstone dust, glass bottles, sticks, sugar, spider eyes, and gunpowder.
Tips: Attack quickly from range or close in before the witch can heal repeatedly.

## Slime
Type: Hostile mob.
Common location: Slime chunks underground and swamp areas under certain conditions.
Behavior: Slimes hop toward players and split into smaller slimes when killed, depending on size.
Drops: Slimeballs from small slimes.
Tips: Slimeballs are useful for sticky pistons, leads, and other crafting recipes.

## Phantom
Type: Hostile undead flying mob.
Common location: Overworld night sky when players have not slept for several in-game days.
Behavior: Phantoms fly and dive toward players from above.
Drops: Phantom membranes.
Tips: Sleep regularly to prevent phantom spawning. Phantom membranes can repair elytra and brew slow falling potions.

## Drowned
Type: Hostile undead aquatic mob.
Common location: Oceans, rivers, and underwater ruins.
Behavior: Drowned attack players in water and sometimes on land. Some can carry tridents.
Drops: Rotten flesh, copper ingots rarely, nautilus shells rarely, and tridents from trident-carrying drowned.
Tips: Be careful when swimming because trident drowned can deal high damage.

## Husk
Type: Hostile undead mob.
Common location: Desert biomes.
Behavior: Husks are zombie variants that do not burn in sunlight. Their attacks can inflict hunger.
Drops: Rotten flesh and rare zombie-like drops.
Tips: Fight them like zombies, but remember sunlight will not kill them.

## Stray
Type: Hostile undead mob.
Common location: Cold and snowy biomes.
Behavior: Strays are skeleton variants that shoot arrows of slowness.
Drops: Bones, arrows, and sometimes arrows of slowness or bows.
Tips: Use a shield and avoid being slowed in open areas.

## Pillager
Type: Hostile illager mob.
Common location: Pillager outposts, patrols, and raids.
Behavior: Pillagers attack using crossbows.
Drops: Arrows, crossbows, and ominous bottles in some versions or raid-related items depending on version.
Tips: Use shields to block crossbow shots and avoid starting raids unless prepared.

## Vindicator
Type: Hostile illager mob.
Common location: Woodland mansions and raids.
Behavior: Vindicators attack with axes and deal high melee damage.
Drops: Emeralds and sometimes axes.
Tips: Keep distance because their melee attack is dangerous.

## Evoker
Type: Hostile illager mob.
Common location: Woodland mansions and raids.
Behavior: Evokers summon vexes and use fang attacks.
Drops: Totem of Undying and emeralds.
Tips: Prioritize killing evokers quickly because vexes can overwhelm the player.

## Vex
Type: Hostile flying mob.
Common location: Summoned by evokers.
Behavior: Vexes fly through blocks and attack players with small swords.
Drops: Usually no useful drops.
Tips: Kill the evoker quickly to prevent more vexes from being summoned.

## Ravager
Type: Hostile raid mob.
Common location: Raids.
Behavior: Ravagers are large, powerful mobs that charge and knock back players.
Drops: Saddles.
Tips: Fight from range or use obstacles because ravagers deal heavy damage.

## Blaze
Type: Hostile Nether mob.
Common location: Nether fortresses, especially near blaze spawners.
Behavior: Blazes fly and shoot fireballs.
Drops: Blaze rods.
Tips: Bring fire resistance potions or shields. Blaze rods are needed for brewing and Eyes of Ender.

## Ghast
Type: Hostile flying Nether mob.
Common location: Open areas of the Nether.
Behavior: Ghasts float and shoot explosive fireballs.
Drops: Ghast tears and gunpowder.
Tips: Hit fireballs back toward ghasts or shoot them with a bow.

## Magma Cube
Type: Hostile Nether mob.
Common location: Nether, especially basalt deltas and Nether fortresses.
Behavior: Magma cubes jump toward players and split into smaller cubes when killed.
Drops: Magma cream from small magma cubes.
Tips: Fight carefully because large magma cubes can deal strong damage.

## Wither Skeleton
Type: Hostile undead Nether mob.
Common location: Nether fortresses.
Behavior: Wither skeletons attack with stone swords and inflict the Wither effect.
Drops: Coal, bones, stone swords, and rarely wither skeleton skulls.
Tips: Their skulls are needed to summon the Wither. Use looting to improve skull drop chances.

## Piglin
Type: Neutral Nether mob.
Common location: Nether wastes, crimson forests, and bastion remnants.
Behavior: Piglins are neutral if the player wears gold armor. They become hostile if the player is not wearing gold or opens certain containers near them.
Drops: Gold-related items and equipment depending on gear.
Tips: Wear at least one piece of gold armor. Piglins can barter when given gold ingots.

## Zombified Piglin
Type: Neutral undead Nether mob.
Common location: Nether and occasionally near Nether portals.
Behavior: Zombified piglins are neutral until attacked. If one is attacked, nearby zombified piglins become hostile.
Drops: Rotten flesh, gold nuggets, and sometimes gold ingots or golden swords.
Tips: Do not attack them unless prepared to fight a group.

## Hoglin
Type: Hostile Nether mob.
Common location: Crimson forests and bastion remnants.
Behavior: Hoglins attack players and can knock them back.
Drops: Porkchops and leather.
Tips: Hoglins are afraid of warped fungi and Nether portals.

## Zoglin
Type: Hostile mob.
Common location: Created when a hoglin is brought to the Overworld or End and zombifies.
Behavior: Zoglins attack most nearby mobs, including players.
Drops: Rotten flesh.
Tips: Avoid bringing hoglins to other dimensions unless intentionally creating zoglins.

## Ender Dragon
Type: Boss mob.
Common location: The End.
Behavior: The Ender Dragon flies around the End island, attacks players, and heals from End crystals.
Drops: Experience and access to the end gateway after defeat.
Tips: Destroy End crystals first, bring a bow, water bucket, blocks, food, and good armor.

## Wither
Type: Boss mob.
Common location: Summoned by the player using soul sand or soul soil and wither skeleton skulls.
Behavior: The Wither flies, shoots explosive skulls, and inflicts the Wither effect.
Drops: Nether Star.
Tips: Fight in a controlled area with strong armor, potions, and healing items.

## Warden
Type: Powerful hostile mob.
Common location: Deep Dark biome, summoned by sculk shriekers.
Behavior: The Warden is blind but detects vibrations and smell. It has powerful melee attacks and sonic boom attacks.
Drops: Sculk catalyst.
Tips: Avoid fighting the Warden. Sneak, avoid making vibrations, and escape if it spawns.

## Guardian
Type: Hostile aquatic mob.
Common location: Ocean monuments.
Behavior: Guardians attack with laser beams and can damage players with spikes when attacked.
Drops: Prismarine shards, prismarine crystals, and fish.
Tips: Use water breathing and good armor when exploring ocean monuments.

## Elder Guardian
Type: Hostile boss-like aquatic mob.
Common location: Ocean monuments.
Behavior: Elder guardians inflict Mining Fatigue and attack with lasers.
Drops: Wet sponge and guardian drops.
Tips: Defeat elder guardians to remove the main threat from an ocean monument.

## Shulker
Type: Hostile End mob.
Common location: End cities.
Behavior: Shulkers hide in shells and shoot projectiles that inflict levitation.
Drops: Shulker shells.
Tips: Shulker shells are used to craft shulker boxes. Be careful of levitation near high places.

## Silverfish
Type: Hostile small mob.
Common location: Strongholds and infested blocks.
Behavior: Silverfish attack players and can call nearby silverfish from infested blocks.
Drops: No major useful drops.
Tips: Break infested blocks carefully and use area attacks when many appear.

## Chicken
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Grassy Overworld biomes.
Behavior: Chickens wander, lay eggs, and can be bred with seeds.
Drops: Raw chicken, feathers, and eggs over time.
Tips: Chickens are useful for feathers, food, and egg farms.

## Cow
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Grassy Overworld biomes.
Behavior: Cows can be bred with wheat and milked using a bucket.
Drops: Raw beef and leather.
Tips: Cows are useful for food, leather, and milk.

## Sheep
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Grassy Overworld biomes.
Behavior: Sheep can be bred with wheat and sheared for wool.
Drops: Wool and raw mutton.
Tips: Use shears to get wool without killing the sheep.

## Pig
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Grassy Overworld biomes.
Behavior: Pigs can be bred with carrots, potatoes, or beetroot. They can be ridden with a saddle and controlled using a carrot on a stick.
Drops: Raw porkchop.
Tips: Pigs are a simple food source.

## Horse
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Plains and savanna biomes.
Behavior: Horses can be tamed and ridden. They require a saddle for player control.
Drops: Leather.
Tips: Horses are useful for fast land travel.

## Donkey
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Plains and savanna biomes.
Behavior: Donkeys can be tamed, ridden, and equipped with chests for storage.
Drops: Leather.
Tips: Donkeys are useful for transportation and carrying items.

## Mule
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Created by breeding a horse and a donkey.
Behavior: Mules can be tamed, ridden, and equipped with chests.
Drops: Leather.
Tips: Mules combine transportation with storage utility.

## Wolf
Type: Neutral mob.
Common location: Forest and taiga-like biomes.
Behavior: Wild wolves are neutral. They can be tamed with bones and become dogs that help attack enemies.
Drops: No useful drops.
Tips: Feed tamed wolves meat to heal or breed them.

## Cat
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Villages and swamp huts.
Behavior: Cats can be tamed with raw cod or raw salmon. Creepers and phantoms avoid cats.
Drops: String rarely when killed, but killing cats is not useful.
Tips: Cats are useful for creeper protection around bases.

## Ocelot
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Jungle biomes.
Behavior: Ocelots are shy and can be made to trust the player with raw fish.
Drops: No useful drops.
Tips: Approach slowly and avoid sudden movement.

## Villager
Type: Passive NPC mob.
Common location: Villages.
Behavior: Villagers work at job site blocks, trade with players, sleep in beds, and can breed when conditions are met.
Drops: No drops.
Tips: Villagers are important for trading, farming, iron farms, and village systems.

## Wandering Trader
Type: Passive trading mob.
Common location: Spawns near players with trader llamas.
Behavior: Wandering traders offer random trades and disappear after some time.
Drops: No useful drops directly, but llamas may drop leather if killed.
Tips: They can sell useful rare items like saplings, coral, or other biome-related goods.

## Iron Golem
Type: Neutral utility mob.
Common location: Villages or built by players using iron blocks and a carved pumpkin.
Behavior: Iron golems protect villagers and attack hostile mobs. They become hostile to players who attack villagers or the golem.
Drops: Iron ingots and poppies.
Tips: Iron golems are useful for village defense and iron farms.

## Snow Golem
Type: Utility mob.
Common location: Built by players using snow blocks and a carved pumpkin.
Behavior: Snow golems throw snowballs at hostile mobs and leave snow trails in some biomes.
Drops: Snowballs.
Tips: Snow golems are useful for snow farming and simple mob distraction.

## Bee
Type: Neutral mob.
Common location: Bee nests in flower forests, plains, meadows, and similar biomes.
Behavior: Bees pollinate flowers and produce honey in nests or hives. They attack if their hive is disturbed without protection.
Drops: No useful drops directly.
Tips: Use a campfire under the hive or nest before harvesting honey or honeycomb.

## Fox
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Taiga and snowy taiga biomes.
Behavior: Foxes are fast, shy, and can hold items in their mouth.
Drops: The item they are holding, if any.
Tips: Foxes can be bred with sweet berries or glow berries.

## Rabbit
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Deserts, snowy biomes, and other specific biomes.
Behavior: Rabbits hop around and flee from players.
Drops: Raw rabbit, rabbit hide, rabbit foot rarely.
Tips: Rabbit foot can be used for brewing leaping potions.

## Turtle
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Beaches.
Behavior: Turtles return to their home beach to lay eggs after breeding.
Drops: Seagrass if killed, but turtle scutes are obtained when baby turtles grow up.
Tips: Scutes are used to craft turtle shells.

## Frog
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Swamps and mangrove swamps.
Behavior: Frogs eat small slimes and magma cubes. They produce froglights when eating small magma cubes.
Drops: No normal drops.
Tips: Frog color depends on the temperature of the biome where the tadpole grows up.

## Axolotl
Type: Passive aquatic mob.
Common location: Lush caves.
Behavior: Axolotls attack many aquatic mobs and can play dead to recover health.
Drops: No useful drops.
Tips: Axolotls can help fight underwater mobs.

## Squid
Type: Passive aquatic mob.
Common location: Rivers and oceans.
Behavior: Squids swim in water and release ink particles when attacked.
Drops: Ink sacs.
Tips: Ink sacs are used for black dye and other crafting uses.

## Glow Squid
Type: Passive aquatic mob.
Common location: Dark underwater areas.
Behavior: Glow squids glow visually and release glowing particles.
Drops: Glow ink sacs.
Tips: Glow ink sacs can make sign text glow and craft glow item frames.

## Dolphin
Type: Neutral aquatic mob.
Common location: Oceans.
Behavior: Dolphins swim in groups and can lead players to underwater treasure if fed fish.
Drops: Raw cod.
Tips: Swimming near dolphins can grant Dolphin's Grace, increasing swim speed.

## Salmon
Type: Passive aquatic mob.
Common location: Rivers and cold oceans.
Behavior: Salmon swim in water and can be collected in buckets.
Drops: Raw salmon.
Tips: Salmon are useful as food and for taming cats.

## Cod
Type: Passive aquatic mob.
Common location: Oceans.
Behavior: Cod swim in schools and can be collected in buckets.
Drops: Raw cod.
Tips: Cod are useful as food and for taming cats.

## Pufferfish
Type: Passive/defensive aquatic mob.
Common location: Warm oceans.
Behavior: Pufferfish inflate when players or mobs get close and can poison nearby entities.
Drops: Pufferfish item.
Tips: Pufferfish are used for brewing water breathing potions.

## Tropical Fish
Type: Passive aquatic mob.
Common location: Warm oceans and lush caves.
Behavior: Tropical fish swim in many colors and patterns.
Drops: Tropical fish item.
Tips: Tropical fish can be collected in buckets and used to breed axolotls.

## Allay
Type: Passive helper mob.
Common location: Pillager outposts and woodland mansions.
Behavior: Allays collect matching items after being given an item by the player.
Drops: The item they are holding, if any.
Tips: Allays are useful for item collection systems.

## Camel
Type: Passive rideable mob.
Common location: Desert villages.
Behavior: Camels can be ridden by two players and can dash across gaps.
Drops: No useful drops.
Tips: Camels are tall enough that some melee mobs have difficulty hitting riders.

## Goat
Type: Neutral/passive mob.
Common location: Mountain biomes.
Behavior: Goats can jump high and may ram entities.
Drops: Goat horns when they ram certain blocks, and sometimes no direct useful drops.
Tips: Goat horns are collectible items with different sounds.

## Sniffer
Type: Passive ancient mob.
Common location: Hatched from sniffer eggs found through archaeology-related gameplay.
Behavior: Sniffers search for ancient seeds by sniffing the ground.
Drops: No useful drops.
Tips: Sniffers can help obtain ancient plants such as torchflower seeds and pitcher pods.

## Armadillo
Type: Passive mob.
Common location: Warm biomes such as savannas and badlands.
Behavior: Armadillos can roll up when threatened. They drop or provide scutes used for wolf armor.
Drops: Armadillo scutes.
Tips: Armadillo scutes are useful for crafting and repairing wolf armor.

## Bogged
Type: Hostile undead skeleton variant.
Common location: Swamps, mangrove swamps, and some trial chamber areas depending on version.
Behavior: Bogged attack with bows and shoot poisoned arrows.
Drops: Bones, arrows, and sometimes poison arrows or bows depending on version.
Tips: Use shields and avoid being poisoned during combat.

## Breeze
Type: Hostile mob.
Common location: Trial chambers.
Behavior: Breezes move quickly and attack with wind charges that create knockback and interact with some blocks.
Drops: Breeze rods depending on version.
Tips: Stay mobile and watch for wind charge knockback in trial chambers.
